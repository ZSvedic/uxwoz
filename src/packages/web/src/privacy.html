Privacy

UX Woz is run by {{operator}}. This page says what it keeps about you, who else sees it, and for how long.

What is stored

  • Your email, once you register. We use it to send your sign-in code and to answer when you ask for more free screens. No newsletter.
  • What you type and make: your prompts, your mockups and their screens, your comments.
  • Your network address, scrambled, for one day only: it counts the free screens a network may use. Then it is thrown away.
  • Which steps of the app you took (picked a design, registered), and how many people took each step a day.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in. No tracking and no ads.

Who else handles it

  • Railway, the host that runs the server and keeps the files.
  • Resend, the mail service that sends the codes and the answers.
  • The model providers that draw the screens: OpenRouter, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude, and OpenAI. They get your prompts and the mockup they belong to, not your email.

How long

  • Without an account, your mockups are deleted 30 days after your last visit.
  • With an account, they stay until you delete it. Delete my account, in the account menu, removes your mockups, your links and your account at once.

Whom to write to

Questions, or a request to see or delete what is kept about you: {{contact}}.
