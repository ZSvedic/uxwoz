UXWoz logo: versions to choose from

One ink colour and one yellow sparkle. Each mark is shown on the night sky, the wood bar, parchment, and in black and white. Tell me a letter for the mark (A, B or C) and a number for the wordmark (1, 2 or 3).

The mark, W+

A: Fredoka's own W, the sparkle as a superscript beside it. B: a W drawn as one rounded line, the sparkle beside it. C: the same W with a shorter last stroke and the sparkle above its tip, like a wand. C is the narrowest, so it is the largest in a favicon.

The wordmark, UXW+oz

1: the sparkle sits between W and o and takes its own room. 2: the sparkle sits in the free room above the small o, so the word is no wider than its letters. 3: as 2, with the drawn W of B and C.
