# Plan: voice that works

This plan owns the fix for voice input: what breaks today, what changes in `voice.ts` and `voice-ui.ts`, and
the check that replays a synthetic voice. It does not own the Edit itself or the sounds of the look.

Status: the user is away and said to execute. The decisions below are the lead's, each with its reason.

## What happens today (measured)

Chrome 157, macOS `say` voices played through the MacBook's speakers into its microphone, every
recognition event logged. Ten voices said *"Hey Woz, use switches"* and *"Hey Woz. Make the title bigger."*

| Chrome heard for "hey Woz" | Clips of 20 | Today's list wakes |
|---|---|---|
| *he was* | 7 | no |
| *hey was* | 4 | yes |
| *they was*, *there was*, *hey* | 1 each | no |
| the two words lost (*"you switchers"*) | 3 | no |
| nothing (a settled piece with no words) | 3 | no |

So the microphone works and Chrome returns words, and 16 times of 20 they do not start with *hey woz*,
*hey was* or *hey waz*. Then nothing shows: no words, no countdown, no pill. That is what the user saw.

Two more findings:

- **Chrome never returns *woz*.** It is not a word Google knows. *"Hey wizard"* came back as *hey wizard*,
  *he wizard* or *very wizard*: the word *wizard* was right in every clip that returned words.
- **Chrome's fake microphone does not reach speech recognition.** With `--use-file-for-fake-audio-capture`
  a page's own recording hears the file only with `--disable-features=AudioServiceSandbox`, and
  recognition returns empty text either way. The check plays the file through the speakers.

The stop phrases: *"Woz stop"* came back as *was stop* or *was stopped*, *"Woz sleep"* as *was sleep*,
*Wesley* or *sleep*. *Stop listening*, *cancel* and *never mind* came back right every time.

## Decisions

1. **The wake phrase stays "hey Woz", and "hey wizard" works too.** A piece wakes when it starts with
   *hey*, *he*, *they* or *there*, then *woz*, *was* or *waz*; or with *wizard* after at most one word.
   Why: it is the smallest change that makes the measured clips work, and *wizard* gives the user a
   phrase Chrome hears right. The cost: talk that starts with *"he was"* wakes it while the microphone
   is on. The three-second countdown with Cancel is the guard, as it is for every Edit.
2. **Show what was heard.** While the microphone is on, a small parchment label above it shows the words
   as Chrome returns them: *Heard: he was you switches*. A settled piece that does not wake adds *Start
   with "hey Woz"*. It fades after four seconds. Why: the user can tell "it hears me but not the phrase"
   from "it hears nothing" without opening anything.
3. **Every stop says why, at once.** *I hear nothing. Check the microphone.* in the label when Chrome
   reports no speech. The red pill at the first error of its kind: no microphone, no network, blocked.
   Retrying up to five times stays.
4. **"Click a screen first." shows on waking**, not three seconds later, when no screen or box is current.
5. **Stop phrases** gain *was stop*, *was stopped*, *was sleep*, *wizard stop*, *wizard sleep*.
6. **The check is a script**, `src/scripts/voice-check.ts`: it starts a separate Chrome with its own
   profile, opens the app, turns the microphone on, plays a `say` clip through the speakers, and prints
   every recognition event, what the label showed and whether the Edit was sent. It stops the Edit's
   request before it reaches a model, so a run costs nothing. It needs a quiet room and speakers that
   the microphone can hear, and it says so when it hears nothing.

## Build

One builder, one critic. Files: `voice.ts` (the lists, a `heard` event), `voice-ui.ts` (the label, the
messages), `design/bar.css` (the label's look), `tests/voice.test.ts` (each heard-as phrase from the table,
the new stop phrases, the `heard` event), `scripts/voice-check.ts`, `spec/behavior.md` (Voice), `README.md`.
The state machine stays free of the DOM.
