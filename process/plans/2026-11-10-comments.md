# Plan: comments beside the screens

This plan owns the new reviewer feedback: a name first, comments pinned to parts of a screen, and where the
owner sees them. It does not own share links, roles, the edit link's budget or the owner's gate: those stay.

Status: the user is away and said to execute. The decisions below are the lead's, each with its reason.

## Example

Ana opens a comment link. She clicks through three screens; nothing asks her anything. On *Nights* she
clicks **Comment**: a card beside the screen asks her name, once. Then *Click the part you mean*: she
clicks the chart, a gold pin **1** lands on it, and a card beside the screen takes her words. She sends
it. The pin and the card stay, and they are there when she reloads.

The owner opens the mockup. *Nights* on the canvas carries pin **1** on the chart. A click on the pin
opens the drawer on **Comments**: *1 · Ana: the bars need a scale*, with **Resolve**. After an Edit that
removes the chart, the comment stays in the list, marked *about an earlier version*, with no pin.

## Decisions

1. **A name first, once.** The first Comment, thumb or Done asks for the name in the margin; the browser
   keeps it. The server refuses feedback and trips without a name, so *Someone* is gone. A trip is sent
   from the moment there is a name, with the screens seen before it. Why trips too: one rule (nothing is
   stored about a nameless visitor) is simpler than two.
2. **Comment is a mode of one click.** **Comment** on the bar, then a click on a part pins the comment
   there; *The whole screen* in the margin pins it to no part. One comment per press, as in Google Docs.
   Why a mode: a click must still open the next screen.
3. **The margin** sits right of the screen, and under it in a narrow window. It lists this screen's
   comments by pin number. Pins are drawn over the screen by the page around it; the frame only reports
   where a part is.
4. **No dialogs.** A dead control opens the same card, pinned to that control, asking *Not designed yet.
   What did you expect here?*; it is stored as a comment. **Done** stays as the ember button and asks
   *Would you use this? Why or why not?* in the margin. Thumbs stay: they are the one-click signal.
5. **Comments only this round, and the owner resolves.** A reviewer sees their own comments (and whether
   one is resolved) on reload; they do not see other reviewers'. Why: a potential customer who reads the
   others' comments first is no longer a fresh pair of eyes, and replies need moderation nobody asked
   for. Nothing polls: comments load with the page.
6. **The owner sees pins on the canvas and the comments in the drawer.** Every screen with open comments
   shows their pins on its parts. A third drawer tab, **Comments**, lists them for the current screen
   with the reviewer's name; a pin's click opens it at that comment, and pointing at a comment rings its
   pin. Why the drawer and not a column on the canvas: the drawer already sits beside the current screen,
   and the tree layout stays untouched. The *Feedback* pane stays as the index: a line jumps to its
   screen and comment. Info keeps the thumbs only.
7. **An anchor is the part's words, its tag and its place among equals.** `about` (as today), `tag`, and
   `nth` among the parts with the same tag and words. The frame looks for the same tag and words, then
   the same words under any tag. No match: *about an earlier version*, no pin. Why not a CSS path: the
   model rewrites the markup around a part on every Edit, and the words are what survive.
8. **Caps stay**: 500 per kind, 1,000 characters. Old feedback loads; entries without an id get one.

## Data and API

```
Feedback  + id, tag?, nth?, resolved? (when)          kind 'dead' is read, no longer written
POST /api/s/:token/feedback    needs name; returns {ok, id}
GET  /api/s/:token/feedback?visitor=   that visitor's comments and thumbs
POST /api/s/:token/trip        needs name
POST /api/feedback {app, id, resolved}   the owner only (an edit link is refused)
frame → page   part {text, tag, nth, x, y, w, h} on a pick and with each action
page → frame   locate [{id, text, tag, nth}]  →  located [{id, x, y, w, h}] (missing: gone)
```

## Build

Two builders, each with a critic.

1. **The reviewer's side**: `core/frame.ts` (part and locate), `core/cache.ts` (fields, ids on load),
   `share.ts`, a new `pins.ts` (anchors and the pin layer, shared with the app), `player.html`,
   `player.ts`, `design/player.css`, spec and tests.
2. **The owner's side**: pins on the canvas, the Comments tab, Resolve, the index that jumps:
   `sharing.ts`, `drawer.ts`, `canvas.ts` (the layer and the message only), `index.html`, `design/`.
