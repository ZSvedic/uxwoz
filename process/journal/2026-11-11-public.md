# 2026-11-11: the public app is built, and stays off

Everything in `process/plans/2026-11-11-public-users.md` is built and merged on `main`: starters,
the visitor's meter, sign-up by code, quota requests, the admin's page, the hints. Nothing is pushed.
The public app is behind `UXWOZ_PUBLIC=1`, which is not set anywhere, so a push changes one thing for
the owner: Starters take the place of Templates. 330 tests pass, the typecheck is clean.

## Your checklist

1. **Say yes to the push.** It deploys dark: private as today, with the starters in the panel.
2. **Resend**: a free account, the domain `mail.uxwoz.com` (Auto Configure writes GoDaddy's records),
   a sending-only key.
3. **Railway variables**: `RESEND_API_KEY`, `UXWOZ_ADMIN_EMAIL` (where requests reach you),
   `UXWOZ_OPERATOR` and `UXWOZ_CONTACT` (the privacy page's name and address). Last, `UXWOZ_PUBLIC=1`.
4. **Before the flag, on the live host**: open `/admin` and read *Your address as the server sees it*.
   It must be your own address, and stay your own when a request forges `X-Real-IP`. If it is wrong,
   set `UXWOZ_ADDRESS_HEADER`. Until this is checked the per-address limits are not to be trusted, and
   only the $3 a day and $20 a month caps hold.
5. **OpenRouter's activity page**: confirm a design bills about $0.0008.

## What a stranger gets

- **Eight starters**, each a recording replayed at its first speed: Home and every screen Home links
  to, four designs each. Picks, clicks and Regenerate there call no model. Recorded live for $0.15 in
  all; 512 KB in `recordings/`.
- **$0.10 as a visitor** (about 14 screens), **$0.15 more** 12 hours after verifying an email, or when
  you press Approve now. Haiku only. Past that, *Ask for more*, which you answer on `/admin`.
- **Five hints**, each shown once at the moment it applies, and a sixth the first time a design is live.
- **An account button** with the free screens left, Register, Sign out, Delete my account, Privacy.

Measured in the app with real calls: a starter cost $0, an own prompt's two designs $0.0014, an Edit
with its filing $0.0017.

## How it holds

- **Each person has their own cache file** and a request can reach no other. A critic found no way
  across.
- **Every live call is checked when it starts**, against the person, the visitor's address, the day and
  the month, with $0.004 held until its cost arrives. The first critic spent $0.30 on a $0.001
  allowance with 30 warm-ups at once, and $0.27 on $0.02 through a race; both are tests now and hold.
- **Sign-in**: an email gets 5 codes and 15 wrong tries a day, one code a minute; a network 10 codes
  and 3 new accounts a day. A code whose mail failed is deleted. The second critic took over an account
  by guessing while the mail service refused; that is a test now and holds.
- **Blocking sticks to the email**, so deleting the account does not undo it.

## How it was built

Ten builders and three critics (Opus), each builder in a worktree, three at a time: about 2 hours
from the go-ahead, about 35 percentage points of the 5-hour window. The critics found eleven real defects;
looking at the app found two more (a first-time visitor's starter click answered 401; the page never
counted *arrived*). 68 files, 5,900 lines added, 134 new tests.

## Not verified

- **A real host.** No push, no Docker build (Docker is not running here). Production mode ran locally.
- **A real email.** Resend was never called; tests fake it, and the local walk read the code from the
  log.
- **Railway's address header** (the checklist's step 4).
- **Narrow screens**: the account card and menu were checked at 720 px by a builder, the starter cards
  not below desktop width.
- **A box's Edit or Visit now refused for lack of free screens**, and Delete my account, in a browser.
  The routes are tested.
- **One test is timing-based** (`bursts.test.ts`, the Regenerate race). It failed once in 31 full runs
  and retries its setup now; which line failed that once is not known.

## Left open

- An attacker can lock any email out of sign-in for a day (15 wrong tries). That is the price of
  capping guesses.
- A call that costs more than the $0.004 held passes a cap by its real cost. A fixer's visit can cost
  $0.01.
- Emails other than codes are counted after they go, so a burst can pass the day's 100 by a few.
- Once the card shows the code step, a mistyped email cannot be changed until the tab closes or the
  code expires.
