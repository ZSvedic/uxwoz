# 2026-11-24: the mobile-first rework, built in a day

The app was rebuilt around one mock at a time: a top bar, cards that flip, four buttons under the
mock, a tour for newcomers. It is on branch `rework-spec`, 398 tests green, not pushed and not
deployed. The plan and the open points are in
[process/plans/2026-11-24-rework-plan.md](../plans/2026-11-24-rework-plan.md).

## Your checklist

1. **Walk the tour on a real phone.** One finger must scroll a mock, two must move the canvas. No
   emulation can prove it.
2. **Hold 🎙️ and talk** in Edit, on Chrome. No critic had a microphone.
3. **Read the tour's words** in `tour/*.html`. The last button says *Try another example* to a
   visitor who has tried none.
4. **Judge the flip on a laptop-size mock.** If it feels heavy it becomes a fade, which is a design
   change, so it waits for your word.
5. **Say yes to the merge into `main`**, which deploys. Back up first, as always.

## What changed for a person

- **A first visit opens the tour**: four mocks Haiku drew from Zel's words, read from `tour/`.
- **One design a mock.** 🎲 Another draws a second, clearly different one. A third press asks for
  better words.
- **A mock is a frame of fixed size** and scrolls inside. Four sizes; the window offers one, and
  New project's *Design for* picks another.
- **Edit, History and Info flip the mock** in place. History has Undo, Redo and every version.
- **The bar is at the top**: the logo (the menu), ←, the name (the dropdown with the boxes and the
  tree), →, the zoom. A laptop adds the boxes and − + ⛶.
- **A click glides** the canvas to the next mock while the model draws it.
- **Filing starts when you click on from a mock**, since nobody picks a design any more.
- **Voice is hold-to-talk** beside every field. The wake phrase is gone.
- **Share and Feedback are pages of the menu.** A mock's comments are part of its Info.
- **The logo is C2**, and the name is *UXWoz* in one word.

## How it was built

Ten builders (Opus), each in a worktree, three at a time at most, in five waves: deletions;
cache, engine, logo; server and frame; canvas, bar and menu; cards and boxes, sharing. Then one
builder for the critics' fixes. About three hours from the yes, with the Mac asleep for part of it.

Five critics: one read the server diff, one the browser diff, three walked the public app in the
browser pane (a phone visitor, a laptop visitor, an owner with a reviewer). Together they reported
about 60 things in work the builders had called done. The worst:

- A first-time visitor could not load the tour at all on the public app.
- An edit link could spend the owner's credits past its budget, and file into another project.
- A newcomer's first project showed the tour's rules and data.
- A box was wider than a phone's screen.
- Pin numbers changed when a comment was resolved.

Model money: 21 cents for the 28 recordings, under a cent for the tour, a few cents for the
cassettes and the walks. The plan said $5 for the recordings; a file costs under a cent.

## Decisions made on the way

- **Routes and files kept their names** (`/api/screen`, `app`): only what a person reads changed.
- **The tour's one component is Bill**, not the big button: Bill is the part two mocks share.
- **The Model picker is the owner's wherever he is the owner**, the public app included. The plan
  said the private app only.
- **A comment's number** is its place by time among all the mock's comments, resolved or not, so a
  number never changes.
- **A replay test and the tour's test** check shape, not words, so `tour/` can be edited by hand.

## Not verified

- **A real touch screen**, iOS Safari, Firefox.
- **A real microphone** for hold-to-talk.
- **Clicks inside a mock**: the pane cannot click a scaled frame, so critics sent the frame's
  message by script.
- **The confirm boxes** of Start over, Delete and Revoke; saved downloads; the clipboard.
- **The resend after sign-up** for a refused Draw again; running out of an edit link's budget.

## Left open

- A reload counts as a new trip. A revoked link's page is bare. Start over gives the project a new
  address. All three were so before.
- Far zoomed out (13%) a name tag still cuts its name.
- Start over and Delete ask in the browser's own box; Delete this mock asks in its card.
- One slip of mine: for a minute a server ran against the everyday cache in `temp/`. It saved
  nothing.
