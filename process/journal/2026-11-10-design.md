All five phases of [the integration plan](../plans/2026-11-10-design-integration.md) are on `main`: the
look lives in `src/packages/web/src/design/`, the app, the player and the sheet at `/design` read the same

`process/design/variant-f.html`, so that file stays.

## Where the app differs from F

By the plan's nine decisions: the logo sits at the left of the bar's strip; a pick is a click on a side
with the Pick pill on hover; the app's words stay; text on ember is dark brown and the light wood is
darker; the drawer rests level and Components never tilts; the zoom menu is the app's.

Found while building:

- A name chip is level and its *L2* is not ember: the name is one text node in `canvas.ts`.
- Ropes leave a screen 40 px from its left edge and end above the child's name, as the old lines did.
- Ember text on parchment (Nav's path, *new*) is a darker ember, `#a83a0a` by day: F's fails contrast.
- Fields are indigo in dark mode, not F's ivory.
- The picked screen ends where it started, not 6 px higher.
