# 2026-11-04: one flow, A OR B, a screen rail, one toolbar

The plan in [process/plans/2026-11-04-one-flow.md](../plans/2026-11-04-one-flow.md), done end to
end. Stacks, tabs, Compare, the slide, lineage arcs and the floating tree confused people; now only
screens the user picked exist.

## What changed

- **A OR B.** Every new screen streams two designs side by side with a big OR between them and one
  sentence above each naming its idea. A click on a finished side keeps it as the screen's first
  version; the other is gone. The view before the A OR B comes back after the pick, and the picked
  screen scrolls into view.
- **Versions instead of variants.** A screen has versions; the last one shows. Edit (the model
  gets the screen and the change) and Regenerate (from the screen's prompt) each add one in place;
  Undo drops the newest. Letters are gone: names are *L2 Camera*.
- **Canvas.** `canvas.ts` replaces `feed.ts` and `tree.ts`: every screen on one pan-and-zoom
  surface, as a tree, one row per level. A screen's first child sits straight below it; later
  children start new columns. One thin line per parent and child. Names, ideas and rails keep their
  size on screen at any zoom.
- **Rail and drawer.** Right of each screen: ✎ Edit, ↻ Regenerate, ↶ Undo, ⓘ Info, ✕ Delete, at
  40 px on screen. Edit and Info open a docked 360 px drawer; the canvas narrows and keeps the
  screen in view.
- **Toolbar.** One 44 px bar: project menu (New mockup, Templates, Recent mockups, Start over,
  Delete), Nav (every screen as an indented tree), zoom (−, a dropdown with Figma's keys, +, fit
  screen). Cost and timing moved into Info and a status pill at the bottom of the canvas.
- **Prompt.** The model now adds `<meta name="idea" content="...">` after its `<title>`. Cassettes
  re-recorded.
- **API.** `/api/screen` streams the A OR B and stores each side as it finishes; new `/api/pick`,
  `/api/revise`, `/api/undo`; `/api/delete` takes a screen key. `/api/choose` and every variant
  field are gone.
- **Migration.** The dev cache went from 31 screens to 29: each screen kept its pick; screens only
  other variants led to were dropped. The old file stays as `screen-cache.json.before-versions`.

## Decisions made here, and where they moved

- Steps 1 and 2 share a commit: the new API broke the old front end, so neither typechecked alone.
- **The A OR B fits by width, top first**, not by its whole height. Both designs grow while they
  stream; a fit by height would jump, or leave the screens tiny. A plain Fit screen still fits the
  whole height.
- **A pick while the other side streams aborts its request**, so its model call stops and costs
  less, instead of finishing and being dropped. The server still drops a side that finishes after a
  pick, for a second browser.
- **One screen at a time.** While a screen is being made or changed, a click that would open
  another says so in the status pill. Simpler than parallel A OR Bs, and the plan's example never
  needs two.
- **✕ under the OR** drops both designs (and the mockup, for the first screen): there was no way to
  say "neither".
- **Edit and Regenerate keep the screen's device**, whatever the Device picker says.
- The current screen is remembered per mockup in this browser, in place of the saved rows.
- The edited-prompt field on versions went with "start over from an edited prompt".

## After the critic

A critic subagent reviewed the whole diff. Fixed:

- A pick while the other side streamed dropped the A OR B from the page before the server
  answered, so the picked frame loaded again at the wrong height. The A OR B now stays drawn until
  the pick comes back, and a second quick click no longer picks again.
- A click into a screen moved the keyboard focus into its frame, so zoom keys and Esc stopped
  working. The frame script now hands them on.
- At a low zoom, rails covered the next column and the OR outgrew its gap; both gaps now grow with
  the rails' size on the canvas, and Fit counts the rail. Zooming around the pointer drifted up or
  down, because rows move as the names keep their size; it now follows the nearest screen.
- A failed side showed "not finished" once stored, instead of its error.
- A side finishing after Start over or a drop (even before the screen existed) could bring the
  A OR B back. The cache now remembers when each key was deleted, and the server checks that.
  A click from a screen that is gone is refused before any model call.
- A migration that failed halfway kept half the screens in memory.
- A reload showed the generate panel; it now reopens the open mockup, A OR B included, without ever
  starting a design by itself.
- New tests in `races.test.ts` for a side finishing after its pick, its mockup's delete, and a drop.

Left as is: the stream's `variant` field (the plan names the sides that way), and the link a click
records for a new screen, beside its parent.

## Feedback round a-g

After trying it with a snoring tracker.

- **Generate panel (a, b).** The Model list lost "(cost est.)"; Model, Device and Theme sit in one
  row, a large Generate below.
- **Device (c).** Opening a mockup now sets the picker to the device of its first screen, for that
  mockup only; New mockup and closing it bring back the saved choice. Generate saves all three
  pickers again, in case a change event was missed. Most likely cause of the report: the snoring app
  was a desktop mockup (its A OR B was at 45%), and Start over used whatever the picker said.
- **Refit (d).** Both designs were fitted at the device's full width while streaming and shrank once
  finished. Once both report their final size, the A OR B is fitted again (height included, but a
  tall pair keeps a readable zoom), unless the user scrolled, zoomed, used a fit or Nav meanwhile.
  The same goes for opening a mockup, and for the first screen of a new one, which has no earlier
  view to go back to.
- **No status pill (e).** Only errors show, and they clear when the next screen or change starts.
  A click that would start a second screen shows a short note, so it does not look broken.
- **Back made a new home screen (f).** The model gave the Back button a long prompt for the home
  screen, so the click made *L3 Snoring tracker home*. The prompt now asks for `data-action="back"`,
  and the app sends any click labelled Back (or *Back to …*, or an arrow alone) to the screen that
  opened this one, and Home-like labels to the first screen, whatever the prompt says. Cassettes
  re-recorded. The L3 already in the cache stays until deleted.
- **Ideas (g).** Up to two lines under the name, then **more**; below 50% zoom only names show.

A critic subagent checked a-g and found more of the same kinds, all fixed: the device was saved
again on opening a mockup, user fits did not count as moving the view, an old error stayed after a
success, the blocked click was silent, Back/Home labels with icons in front were missed, the model
list was cut at 11em, the first pick of a new mockup went back to the previous mockup's zoom, an
opened mockup was fitted before its size was known, and an opened idea stayed open after an Edit.

## Back, three whys; one generate panel

- **Why did Back make a new screen?** Its action prompt matched no stored key, so the app asked
  for a new screen.
- **Why did it match nothing?** The model wrote a long description of the home screen. The prompt
  had a word for the first screen (`home`) but none for "the screen before", and the first screen
  is not in the list of existing prompts the model copies from.
- **Why did a model's wording decide this?** The app already knows where Back goes (the parent),
  but left navigation entirely to the prompt string, with no check of the label and no test with a
  Back button. Fixed in the round above: the label and a `back` action decide, and a test covers
  the labels.

The generate panel had two looks: alone with Templates and Recent, or a smaller popover without
them, because the project menu had its own copies. Now there is one panel, the same in both cases,
and the project menu only has New mockup, Start over and Delete.

## Names for links, levels worked out

The mitigation from 7911bc3 (Oct 24) was prompt-only: write `home`, copy listed prompts exactly.
Nothing checked it, the prompt had no word for "the screen before", the first screen was not in
the list to copy from, and its test passed if any one link matched. Since then more controls per
screen (320f000) and two designs per screen raised the odds of a reworded one.

- **Names.** A picked screen gets a fixed name from its title, unique in the mockup (`sleep-report`;
  a clash takes words from its prompt, then `-2`); the first screen is `home`. Info shows it.
- **Tree in each request.** The model gets the picked screens as an indented tree,
  `@name: the prompt that made it`, and links with `data-action="@name"` or `back`. In the
  re-recorded cassettes it writes `@home`, `@cinnamon-rolls`, `back`; a test fails on any unknown
  name.
- **Levels are the fewest clicks from Home**, worked out on every draw (and on the server) from
  each screen's first click and the buttons in its newest version. An Edit on Home that adds a
  button to Order tracking moved it from L4 to L2; Undo moved it back. The canvas hangs each screen
  under a screen one click nearer Home, preferring the one that made it.
- **Removed:** stored levels, recorded clicks, `/api/link`, and the re-parenting code. Deletes use
  the same tree. Back goes to the screen it hangs under.
- **Cache file:** version 3. Version 2 files get names on load; older files are moved aside (the
  dev cache was already version 2). A name freed by a delete can be taken again, so old `@settings`
  buttons find a new Settings screen.

A critic subagent found no blocking bugs; fixed: Back followed the first click instead of the tree,
the link finder missed nested controls and quoted `>`, names repeated a shared subject
(`q3-pricing-review-2`), and a long tree lost whole branches at the 40-line cap (now the deepest
levels go first).

## Planned screens, recent mockups, back and forward, packed rows

**Why duplicates kept coming back** (NiceSnore had *Remedies log* and *Remedies*): a link was a
sentence, and only the exact same sentence found a screen. Two ways in: an Edit ("Remedies should
be a separate screen") added *All remedies* next to the old *Remedies* button, with a new sentence;
and screens made before the target existed (Morning summary, Log a remedy, Snore history) each had
their own sentence for it. The tree in the request only knew screens already made.

- **Every link names its screen**: `data-action="@remedies-log: prompt"`, or `@name` alone for a
  screen the mockup has. The name decides; the prompt is only for making it. Cache key
  `root » @name`.
- **Planned screens**: names that picked screens link to but nobody opened yet. Worked out, not
  stored (`plannedScreens`); in the tree the model gets (marked *not made yet*, now 80 lines), in
  Nav (greyed; a click makes one), and gone with their last link.
- **Prompt**: one screen one name, one name one screen. The first recording gave four breads one
  `@bread-detail`; the rule now names `@latte-details` / `@mocha-details`. Luna sometimes writes a
  bare new name, so a missing prompt comes from the planned link, else the name's words and the
  button's.
- Considered and left out: an outline-first screen (fixes only what is planned up front), a model
  check on each click (a second call; kept as the fallback if names still drift), background
  rewriting of old screens (as costly as making them again, and changes their design).
- Old prompt-only links still work by exact prompt. One NiceSnore link (`@remedy-log`, made before
  names were kept from links) was repointed by hand in the dev cache to `@log-remedy`; backup in
  `temp/screen-cache.backup-2026-11-04-names.json`.

Also: the project menu lists the last ten mockups; **← →** around Nav walk the screens looked at
(⌘[ ⌘], mouse buttons); rows are packed side by side, a first child under its parent when free,
and each parent's lines branch at their own height below the row (leftmost lowest), entering
children further right than they leave parents, so lines never cross or run together. The first
try had a parent's line and another parent's child's line on one vertical; seen on a test mockup,
fixed by the different entry point.

A critic subagent found one real bug: deleting a named screen that Home still linked to kept its
children, hung under the empty planned box (the planned screen gave them a way in). Planned screens
are now leaves. Also fixed: the delete dialog counted planned screens; the 80-line tree dropped made
screens before planned ones; the mouse back button over a screen left the page; an old prompt link
missed a named screen made from the same prompt; a planned screen labelled like Home could not be
made from Nav; the model now sees "(this screen)"; the project menu no longer redraws while open.
Left as is: a screen named from its title at the pick may take a planned name (then links to that
name open it, which is the point).
